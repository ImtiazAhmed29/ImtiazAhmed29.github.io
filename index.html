Bio

I'm an NYC-based mechanical engineering student at The Cooper Union focused on aerospace vehicle design, particularly aerodynamics and structures. I'm also interested in flight dynamics and control, especially where these disciplines interact with the physical design of an aircraft or spacecraft.

My work spans mechanical design, CAD, fabrication, sensing, and controls, with an emphasis on building and testing physical systems. I've worked on Formula SAE structures, environmental sensing hardware, and CubeSat attitude determination and control systems. Through these projects, I enjoy taking a system from an initial concept through design, fabrication, testing, and iteration.
